01 / Method

Actions describe motion.
Process forecasts give it context.

A vision-language-action model can propose several plausible actions from the same observation. The challenge is choosing an action sequence that advances the task.

PatchVLA gives each action candidate a process forecast. Predicted phases, local progress, and boundaries connect action generation to sequence evaluation and critic-guided latent search.

One representation,
three connected decisions.

Each candidate carries its own forecast through generation, evaluation, and refinement.

  1. 01

    Generate & forecast

    Generate action candidates together with forecasts of phase, local progress, and boundaries.

    PhaseProgressBoundary
  2. 02

    Evaluate the sequence

    A sequence critic combines the observation, actions, and process forecast to score each candidate.

    observation + actions + process → score
  3. 03

    Refine & execute

    Critic-guided latent MPPI refines the proposals. The policy selects a candidate, acts, and replans.

    latent search → select → act
How is PatchVLA trained?+

Expert-guided VLM annotation supplies process labels. The policy learns actions and process forecasts; candidate outcomes train the sequence critic.

02 / Results

Main results
and ablations.

Reported comparisons on LeHome-Fold and LIBERO-Long.

Bold marks the best value; underline marks the next distinct value within each table. Ties share rank. ↑ Higher is better. ↓ Lower is better.

LeHome-Fold: 240 trials across four garment types, with 200 on seen garments and 40 on unseen garments. LIBERO-Long: ten tasks with 50 initial states per task.

Evaluation protocol

Tables report the supplied evaluation summaries. LeHome-Fold repeat selection and run-level split mapping have not been reconciled across methods. LIBERO-Long outcome and command metrics are reported as separate summaries; their execution-level pairing is not verified.

The one-round selection and full-controller comparisons use eight candidate evaluations per decision. The full LIBERO controller reuses latent proposals; reuse in the one-round setting is unspecified. These rows compare controller configurations.

SR, NGC, NR-SR₂, NG-AUC, and PG-AUC use a 0–100 scale. Jump and Δa² report command differences as MSE in units of 10⁻³. X-VLA command metrics are unavailable because its action representation is incompatible.

Metric definitions
NR-SR₂
Successful episodes without losing an acquired new goal; acquisition and loss each require two consecutive samples.
NGC
Final fraction of newly required goal predicates satisfied.
NG-AUC
New-goal completion accumulated over the episode control budget.
PG-AUC
Credits acquisitions persisting until observed termination.
Jump
Replan-boundary consecutive-command differences over the same six continuous action components.
Δa²
Consecutive-command roughness over the same six continuous action components.
