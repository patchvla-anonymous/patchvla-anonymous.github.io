01 / The idea

Actions describe motion.
Process forecasts give it context.

From the same observation, a generative vision-language-action model can propose several plausible action chunks. The decision is which candidate advances the task through the right sequence of interactions.

PatchVLA connects generation and evaluation with candidate-aligned process forecasts. For each proposed action sequence, it predicts interaction phase, local progress, and subtask boundaries. A sequence critic evaluates the resulting action–process trajectory and guides a search over the policy’s latent proposals.

02 / The method

One representation,
three connected decisions.

Each candidate carries its own forecast through generation, evaluation, and refinement.

  1. 01

    Generate & forecast

    A process-conditioned flow policy proposes action chunks. For each chunk, an aligned forecast describes phase, local progress, and boundary probability.

    PhaseProgressBoundary
  2. 02

    Evaluate the sequence

    A sequence critic combines observation, candidate actions, and predicted process to evaluate each proposed continuation.

    observation + actions + process → score
  3. 03

    Refine & execute

    Critic-guided, MPPI-like updates refine the latent proposal distribution. The controller selects a complete evaluated chunk, executes a short prefix, and replans.

    latent search → intact candidate → action prefix
How is PatchVLA trained?+

Sparse expert anchors and offline VLM-assisted stage labeling provide process supervision. Actor training learns action generation and process prediction; recorded candidate outcomes then supervise the sequence critic. The action generator stays frozen during planning.
