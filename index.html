01 / The idea

Actions describe motion.
Process forecasts give it context.

A vision-language-action model can propose several plausible actions from the same observation. The challenge is choosing an action sequence that advances the task.

PatchVLA gives each action candidate a process forecast. Predicted phases, local progress, and boundaries connect action generation to sequence evaluation and critic-guided latent search.

02 / The method

One representation,
three connected decisions.

Each candidate carries its own forecast through generation, evaluation, and refinement.

  1. 01

    Generate & forecast

    Generate action candidates together with forecasts of phase, local progress, and boundaries.

    PhaseProgressBoundary
  2. 02

    Evaluate the sequence

    A sequence critic combines the observation, actions, and process forecast to score each candidate.

    observation + actions + process → score
  3. 03

    Refine & execute

    Critic-guided latent MPPI refines the proposals. The policy selects a candidate, acts, and replans.

    latent search → select → act
How is PatchVLA trained?+

Expert-guided VLM annotation supplies process labels. The policy learns actions and process forecasts; candidate outcomes train the sequence critic.
